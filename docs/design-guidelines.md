# Website design preferences

- Use warm grey, cream, charcoal and muted neutral accents. Avoid blue text or default blue links in new website UI.
- Keep link states and keyboard focus visible through underlines, borders and neutral contrast.
- Use natural, specific labels such as "Our research", "Publications" and "Get in touch". Avoid repeated generic labels such as "Explore this theme".
- Research themes, their full descriptions and acknowledgements belong on Home. Preserve old `/research` URLs as redirects to the corresponding Home anchors.
- For prominent Home sections, take inspiration from the strong heading hierarchy, short rules, generous spacing and contrasting sections of https://mjcarpentryandjoinery.com.au/. Keep this website's own palette and content.
